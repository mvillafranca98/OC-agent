# Signal library — Mortgage Signals Agent

Stable reference for signal categories. Keep this file cache-friendly; avoid frequent edits.

---

## A) MLO “ready to move” signals

Use these categories when scoring mortgage loan officers for job-change propensity. Each signal should be recorded with: type, description, evidence_url, snippet, timestamp, confidence (e.g. low / medium / high).

| Category | Description | What to look for |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Profile changes** | Title, headline, or profile edits | Recent changes to LinkedIn title, headline, or “About”; frequent profile edits in a short window |
| **Open to work** | Job-seeking language | “Open to work,” “Exploring opportunities,” “Open to roles,” or similar explicit language on profile or posts |
| **Posting frequency (career)** | Increased posting about career dissatisfaction | More frequent posts about workload, culture, compensation, or desire for change |
| **Recruiter engagement** | Observable recruiter contact | Public indicators of recruiter outreach or “getting reached out to” (e.g. posts, comments); use only what is clearly public |
| **Team departures** | Colleagues leaving | Mentions of teammates or peers leaving; branch or team churn |
| **Compensation / commission** | Complaints or concerns about pay | Posts or reviews mentioning commission structure, pay cuts, or compensation dissatisfaction |
| **Branch closure proximity** | Office or branch closure | MLO’s branch or office closing, or announced closures affecting their location |
| **Company turmoil** | Employer instability or bad news | Layoffs at employer, bad press, regulatory issues, merger/restructuring that could affect job security or morale |

**Evidence:** Always capture at least: source type (e.g. LinkedIn, news, review site), URL (if available), snippet (short quote or paraphrase), and date/timestamp. Every high-priority lead must have at least 2 sources or 1 high-confidence source with a clear snippet.

## B) Company distress signals

Use these categories when scoring mortgage companies for “distressed or ready to sell” likelihood. Record each signal with type, description, evidence_url, snippet, timestamp, confidence.

| Category | Description | What to look for |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Layoffs / WARN / staff reductions** | Workforce reductions | WARN notices, layoff announcements, “restructuring,” headcount cuts in news or official statements |
| **Branch closures** | Physical location closures | Announced or reported branch closures, market exits |
| **Negative review spike** | Deteriorating employer reputation | Sharp increase in negative Glassdoor, Indeed, or Google reviews; themes like management, pay, stability |
| **Regulatory actions** | Enforcement or regulatory mentions | Fines, consent orders, enforcement actions, NMLS or state regulatory mentions (public record only) |
| **Leadership churn** | C-suite or key exec exits | Multiple or high-profile executive departures in a short period |
| **Acquisition rumors / broker chatter** | M&A or sale speculation | Public rumors, broker or industry chatter about sale or acquisition (public sources only) |
| **Financial stress (news)** | Financial difficulty indicators | News about funding issues, credit lines, losses, or other financial stress indicators |

**Evidence:** Same as MLO: source type, URL, snippet, date. Build a short “distress hypothesis” summary per company listing the main signals and evidence; include in the company lead output.
